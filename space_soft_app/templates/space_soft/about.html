About Us

Spacesoft Integral System Ltd is a forward thinking ICT and energy solution company commited to building smarter, and more connected enviroments. We specializes in Software Development (Website and Mobile Apps). We also offer reliable renewable energy solutions, Like Solar System Installations, and Inverter setup

In a short time, Spacesoft has earned a reputation as one of Nigeria's leading ICT Companies and renewable energy Companies, driven by integrity, cost-innovation, and deep commitment to solving real-world challenges.

We Create, Design, Power, and Connect.

Our Story

Founded by Innocent Alexander, a former lecturer at John Bosco Institute of Technology, Onitsha, Spacesoft was officially incorporated as a Limited Liability Company in 2017. What started as a small ICT consultancy has evolved into a full-fledged tech and energy company with a growing footprint in Nigeria.

Our head office is located in Asaba, Delta State, where our team continue to serve clients with tailored solutions that deliver measurable, long-term impact.

Our Mission

To design, develop, and deliver cutting-edge electronics, sustainable green energy solutions, and locally-driven software innovations that empower Africa’s industries, reduce import dependency, and accelerate the continent’s technological and economic self-reliance.

Our Vision

To be Africa’s leading hub for indigenous innovation in electronics, clean energy, and software technology-driving a future where Africa builds what it needs, powers its growth sustainably, and competes on the global stage with locally developed solutions.
